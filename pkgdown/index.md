  <section class="openspecy-section openspecy-value-section" id="why-openspecy" aria-labelledby="why-openspecy-title">
    <div class="openspecy-value-intro">
      <p class="openspecy-eyebrow">Why OpenSpecy</p>
      <h2 id="why-openspecy-title">One open route from instrument output to interpretable evidence.</h2>
      <p>
        Raman and Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy can reveal a material's
        molecular fingerprint, but file formats, preprocessing choices, and
        closed reference systems make results difficult to compare. OpenSpecy
        brings those steps into a visible, reusable workflow.
      </p>
      <p class="openspecy-science-note">
        OpenSpecy supports scientific judgment; it does not replace instrument
        quality assurance, appropriate controls, or expert review of uncertain
        identifications.
      </p>
    </div>
    <ol class="openspecy-workflow" aria-label="OpenSpecy analysis workflow">
      <li><span>Read</span><p>Bring individual, batch, and map spectra from common instrument formats into one coherent structure.</p></li>
      <li><span>Process</span><p>Apply traceable steps such as smoothing, baseline correction, range restriction, alignment, and normalization.</p></li>
      <li><span>Identify</span><p>Compare spectra with openly managed reference libraries and inspect the evidence behind candidate matches.</p></li>
      <li><span>Share</span><p>Export results and metadata or reproduce the same workflow in R for review, teaching, and collaboration.</p></li>
    </ol>
  </section>
